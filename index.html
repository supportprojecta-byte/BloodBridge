Essential Knowledge

Why Donate Blood with BloodBridge?

Donating blood is safe, simple, and takes just 15 minutes. It stimulates fresh red blood cell production, reduces unwanted iron reserves, and revitalizes your heart health.

1 Donation = 3 Lives

Whole blood is separated into Red Cells, Platelets, and Plasma. Each component can help a different patient fighting critical illness or trauma.

Quick 15 min donation

Strict 18+ Age Eligibility

Donors must be 18 years or older with good general health, a minimum weight of 45 kg, and normal blood pressure to ensure total donor and receiver safety.

Mandatory age verification
